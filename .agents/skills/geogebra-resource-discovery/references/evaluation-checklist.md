# GeoGebra candidate checklist

- The URL is `https://www.geogebra.org/m/<id>`.
- The mathematics matches the exact concept and conventions of the lesson.
- The resource opens as a public material rather than an editor, calculator, login page, or embed endpoint.
- The learner action is explicit: drag, vary, compare, conjecture, or verify.
- The deck remains self-contained if the internet or resource is unavailable.
- The link is optional and does not replace an explanation, proof, example, or required figure.
- The source records author and verified-resource status when visible, without claiming verification when it is unknown.
