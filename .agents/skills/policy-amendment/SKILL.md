# Policy amendment

Material changes to review count, roles, output format, reference access, model default, course MCQ requirement, audience or scope require a structured amendment and updated/reconfirmed TASK.md. The amendment uses no independent hash. Technical lint/logging fixes may be recorded as non-material. `mpres policy audit` must detect split-brain policy files.
