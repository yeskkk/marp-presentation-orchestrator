# Audience channel

Review from the stated audience profile. Perform random-entry checks on concept and continuation slides. Ask whether a learner can identify the object, conditions, units, current question, and relation between table/image and mathematics, without assuming nominal prior exposure equals mastery.

A GeoGebra link must remain optional: a learner who is offline or does not open it still receives the complete in-class explanation and all information needed for the stated outcome.

- Confirm that the deck remains fully understandable offline and that GeoGebra link text tells this audience what to manipulate or observe without making the resource compulsory.

For courses, do not treat “the whole deck cannot fit the nominal minutes” as a finding by itself. The intended policy is a nominal-duration core path followed by optional worked-example extensions; judge whether the stopping point is intelligible and the core path is teachable, not whether every slide can be finished.
